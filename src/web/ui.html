■ Rust Markov Engine · Suno Bark TTS · Twitch IRCv3

A Twitch chat bot that learns from your community and talks back.

Mockbot is a multi-brain Markov chain and Suno Bark TTS bot for Twitch. It listens to your live chat room and builds a statistical word-transition graph from the messages your viewers type. Every sentence it generates is stitched together word-by-word from your own community's vocabulary, emotes, and inside jokes.

Every streamer gets an isolated brain (channel:yourname) with full control over how often the bot speaks, which brains it blends together, blocked words, automatic ban/timeout purges, and optional GPU-accelerated Bark text-to-speech for OBS.

How Markov word-stitching works (Order-2 state crossover)
Two separate chat messages that share a 2-word pair allow the chain to jump tracks mid-sentence:
CHAT MESSAGE #1 (20 minutes ago)
"i swear the best part is eating at taco bell at 3am"
SHARED 2-GRAM STATE
[ eating, at ]
CHAT MESSAGE #2 (5 minutes ago)
"why was everyone eating at the boss room during phase two"
GENERATED OUTPUT → @mockbot: i swear the best part is eating at the boss room during phase two

What Mockbot does

Built in Rust to handle multi-channel Markov generation, live corpus editing, and custom persona mixing.

01 · Isolated Corpora

Per-Channel Brains & Real-Time Blending

Every channel has its own Markov brain (channel:yourname) that learns from incoming chat messages. You can run 100% on your own community's chat, blend multiple brains together with percentage sliders (such as 70% your channel + 30% global), or import historical logs (TwitchDownloaderCLI JSON, plain text, or IRC chat logs) so the bot knows your channel's lore immediately.

02 · Interactive Commands

!speak, !mock @user & Custom Commands

Viewers can run !speak [topic] to generate a sentence seeded around a specific word, or run !mock @username to generate a message built strictly from that single chatter's message history in your channel. Streamers can also define custom !commands mapped to specific brains and seed words.

03 · Anti-Spam Cadence

AND-Gated Cadence & Live Stream Detection

Mockbot won't spam a quiet or offline channel. Automatic messages use an AND gate — requiring both a minimum number of chat lines (default 100 lines) and a minimum cooldown timer (default 15 minutes) to pass before speaking — and pause automatically whenever your Twitch stream is offline.

04 · Stream Events

Channel Points, Bits, Raids & Subs

Hook your Twitch community events directly into the bot: trigger Markov responses or Bark TTS audio clips automatically when someone raids your channel, subscribes, cheers a minimum number of bits, or redeems a specific Twitch Channel Point reward.

Suno Bark TTS & OBS Overlay

Optional GPU-accelerated text-to-speech with custom voice cloning and a browser-source overlay for OBS Studio.

GPU Synthesis · .npz Cloning

Expressive Bark Audio & Voice Cloning

Suno Bark synthesizes natural vocal inflections including laughter ([laughs]), sighs ([sighs]), gasps, and singing (♪). Upload any .wav or .mp3 clip in the dashboard to clone a custom .npz voice preset, enable Voice Drift so the voice mutates over the course of a stream, or save funny Bark hallucinations as permanent voices.

60fps WebAudio RMS · /obs/channel

Volume-Reactive OBS Browser Source

Drop your channel's /obs/yourname URL into OBS as a Browser Source. It plays incoming TTS clips automatically, animates your custom idle/talking PNG or GIF avatar at 60fps using WebAudio RMS volume analysis, and supports instant panic-skipping from the web dashboard or by typing !skip in chat.

Security, Privacy & Moderation

Designed for live Twitch streams where moderation safety, ban enforcement, and channel isolation actually matter.

IRC CLEARCHAT & CLEARMSG

Automatic Ban & Timeout Purge

Mockbot listens to Twitch IRC moderation events in real time. The moment a moderator bans or times out a user — or deletes a single message — Mockbot automatically purges every line learned from that user out of SQLite and rebuilds your channel's Markov brain in memory so deleted content can never be repeated.

Overlap < 70% · Word Blocklists

Blocked Words, Bot Ignore Lists & Anti-Parroting

Incoming chat messages and generated outputs are filtered against both a global blocklist and your channel's custom blocked-words list. URLs and bot commands are stripped before training, known bots (Nightbot, StreamElements, Sery_Bot, etc.) are ignored automatically, and contiguous N-gram overlap checks prevent the bot from parroting a single message verbatim.

Twitch OAuth · Corpus Isolation

Verified Ownership & Private Brains

Channel settings are locked to verified broadcasters via Twitch OAuth (or in-chat broadcaster verification with !mockbot claim). You can enable Private brain in your settings so other streamers cannot blend or !mock your channel's corpus, and lock your OBS overlay URL behind a secret key (?key=...).

Rust Single-Binary · Local SQLite

Open Source & Self-Hosted

Mockbot is a single-binary Rust application (tokio, axum, rusqlite, ratatui) with an embedded SQLite database and local CUDA Python worker for Bark TTS. Inspect the full source code on GitHub (Dusky/mockbot).

Chat commands reference

Commands available in your Twitch chat once @mockbot joins your channel.

Command Permission Description
!speak [topic] Everyone (cooldown) Generate a Markov chat response, optionally seeded with a topic word or phrase.
!mock @username [topic] Everyone (cooldown) Generate a sentence built strictly from a specific chatter's message history in your channel (or another public brain).
!tts <text> Streamer / Mods / Trusted Synthesize text with your channel's Bark TTS voice and play it on your OBS overlay.
!skip Streamer / Mods / Trusted Clear pending TTS clips in the queue and immediately stop any audio playing in OBS.
!mockbot lines <n> Streamer / Mods / Trusted Set the minimum number of chat lines required between automatic messages.
!mockbot time <minutes> Streamer / Mods / Trusted Set the minimum cooldown in minutes between automatic messages.
!mockbot voice <preset> Streamer / Mods / Trusted Change your channel's active Bark TTS voice preset from chat.
!mockbot start / stop Streamer / Mods / Trusted Unmute or mute automatic Markov responses in your channel.
!mockbot part Streamer / Mods Have the bot leave your Twitch chat channel immediately.
